👜 What Is Pouchy?

Have you ever tried to move a file from one folder into an email, or copy a picture into a chat window, and the moment you start dragging, everything goes wrong? A window closes, you click the wrong thing, and the drag is ruined. Pouchy fixes that exact, everyday annoyance.

Pouchy puts a small, friendly drop shelf on top of your screen. Anything you drop onto that shelf just waits there, safely parked, until you are ready to place it somewhere else. Think of it as a little pocket for your mouse. If you have ever used Dropover or Yoink on a Mac, this is the same idea, made natively for Windows.

Pouchy is built with C# and .NET using Windows Presentation Foundation, and it follows the Fluent Design look of modern Windows. That means it feels like part of the system, starts quickly, and does not hog your memory like a browser-based app would.

✨ What Makes It Handy

🫨 Shake to summon

Grab the mouse, give it a quick shake, and the shelf pops up right under your cursor. No keyboard shortcut to memorize.

📦 Park almost anything

Files, folders, images, links and chunks of text can all sit on the shelf together, side by side.

🎯 Drop it anywhere

Drag an item off the shelf straight into Explorer, an email, a chat window, or any app that accepts drops.

⏸️ Pause without losing your place

Park an item, navigate to a different folder or window, then come back to it. The drag is never broken.

⚡ Native and lightweight

Built with .NET and WPF instead of a web wrapper, so it opens fast and stays out of the way of your system resources.

🎨 Fits right in

Rounded corners, soft shadows and a Fluent Design style so the shelf looks like it belongs on Windows 10 and 11.

🖱️ How It Works in Three Steps

  1. Shake your mouse. A quick side-to-side shake brings the Pouchy shelf onto your screen, floating above whatever you are working on.
  2. Drop things onto it. Drag a file, a folder, an image, a link or selected text onto the shelf and let go. The item is parked for as long as you need it.
  3. Drag it back out. When you reach the place you actually wanted, drag the item off the shelf and drop it there. Delete or clear the shelf when you are finished.
